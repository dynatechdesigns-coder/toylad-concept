# Toylad Concept

**Website for a cake, pastry and event business in Abuja, Nigeria.**

Toylad Concept makes custom cakes and pastries and offers event decoration and full event management. This site shows the business's work and lets customers book their celebration directly on WhatsApp.

🔗 **Live site:** https://toylad-concept.vercel.app/

![Toylad Concept homepage](screenshot.png)

## Features

- **Showcase of work:** custom cakes, artisanal pastries and event decoration
- **WhatsApp booking:** customers contact the business directly to get a quote
- **Responsive design:** works on phones, tablets and desktops
- **Private admin page:** the owner logs in and uploads her own cake photos, with no developer needed
- **Brand styling:** warm orange-terracotta theme matching the business identity

## Built with
- MongoDB (admin data)
- Cloudinary (image uploads and hosting)
- Vercel (deployment)
